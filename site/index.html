Station Area Explorer

This is a demonstration built by Jarvus Innovations for its proposal to the Metropolitan Council's Displacement Dashboard RFP. It draws the Council's own published transit stations, lines, and station areas on an open-source map. Its figures are Census Bureau estimates used as placeholders; it is not the Council's product and not its displacement analysis.

Things to try

  • Pick a station area on the map or from the lists to see its figures, a chart, and a data table.
  • Switch the Census period to compare 2010 to 2014, 2015 to 2019, and 2020 to 2024.
  • Open the one-page print view for any station area or line.

The site is a folder of plain files, updated by one command, with no license fees. How this was built
